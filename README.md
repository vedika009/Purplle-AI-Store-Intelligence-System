# Purplle-AI-Store-Intelligence-System
Purplle AI Store Intelligence System from raw CCTV footage

Welcome to Round 2 of the Purplle Tech Challenge 2026.
This round is designed for builders who can think beyond code — engineers who can break down ambiguous problems, make strong architectural decisions, and build production-ready systems under real-world constraints.
Unlike traditional coding rounds, this challenge mirrors the kind of problems solved inside high-scale technology teams every day. You’ll work with raw inputs, imperfect data, operational edge cases, and open-ended engineering decisions — just like in a real production environment.
This year’s challenge focuses on building an AI-powered Store Intelligence System from raw CCTV footage. Participants are expected to design and implement an end-to-end pipeline that combines computer vision, event streaming, real-time analytics, anomaly detection, and production-grade APIs.
You are free to choose your own architecture, models, frameworks, and engineering approach. AI tools are fully allowed — and encouraged. What matters is not just what you build, but how you think, evaluate trade-offs, and explain your decisions.

We are looking for:

Strong problem-solving and system thinking
Practical AI engineering skills
Production-ready implementation quality
Ownership and decision-making ability
Clear technical reasoning and documentation The challenge is intentionally open-ended. There is no single “correct” solution. Ship something you are proud of.
